What Is Appliance Leak Cleanup? (Quick Answer)

Appliance leak cleanup is finding and drying water from a refrigerator, ice maker, dishwasher or other appliance that has spread under flooring, cabinets and into the wall behind it. Because these leaks are often slow and hidden, thermal mapping is used to find the full wet area before drying.

Appliance leak cleanup at a glance:

  • Common sources: plastic or kinked ice maker lines, dishwasher supply valves, drain hoses, door seals
  • Where water hides: under hardwood and LVP, under cabinets and the island, inside the wall behind the fridge
  • Typical drying time: 3 to 5 days; 5 to 8 days with hardwood mat drying
  • Typical estimate: roughly $800 to $2,500 for a small leak affecting the floor only

Fridge or Dishwasher Leaking? Do These 5 Things

  • Check under the kitchen sink first. In many kitchens the ice maker line connects to the cold pipe under the sink with its own valve. Closing it there avoids dragging the fridge across wet wood.
  • Protect the floor before moving the fridge. If the valve is behind the fridge, put plastic gliders or thin plywood under the rollers so they don't dent wet hardwood.
  • Shut off the dishwasher supply. The valve is usually under the sink. Then turn off the dishwasher breaker.
  • Don't run the dishwasher again. A leaking seal, pump or drain hose pushes more water under the floor each cycle.
  • Photograph the line or hose. Split tubing and corroded fittings help show a sudden failure for your adjuster.

Why Appliance Leaks Do So Much Hidden Damage

Appliance leaks are damaging because they're usually slow, hidden behind or under the appliance, and spread under flooring before anyone sees them.

The Fridge Sits in a Hidden Alcove

Built-in and counter-depth fridges in many Willow Bend, Gleneagles and Deerfield kitchens sit in a recessed space. A drip there can run down the wall and under the flooring until boards cup or baseboards swell.

Dishwashers Can Sit Lower Than the Floor

In some remodeled kitchens, new flooring was laid up to the front of the dishwasher, not under it. Leaking water then flows sideways under cabinets and flooring instead of out the front.

Plastic Lines Fail

Plastic ice maker tubing gets brittle and splits. Thin copper coils can kink each time the fridge is pushed back. Both are common in older kitchens.

Where the Water Goes

Appliance leak water spreads into three hidden areas: under the floor, under the cabinets and inside the wall.

  • Under hardwood and LVP: water spreads beneath the planks from the fridge alcove or dishwasher area. See hardwood floor drying.
  • Under cabinets and the island: it runs into the hollow toe-kick spaces. See cabinet drying.
  • Inside the fridge wall: a slow drip soaks the drywall and bottom wood plate behind the fridge and into the next room. See wall cavity drying.

How Appliance Leaks Are Dried in Place

In-place drying follows five steps, used whenever moisture readings show it will work. Work follows recognized restoration practices (IICRC S500 guidance).

  • Move the appliance safely. The fridge is rolled out on floor-protection glides, or the dishwasher is pulled from its opening.
  • Map the wet area. Thermal cameras and meters trace water under floors, cabinets, the island and inside walls.
  • Dry the floor. Hardwood gets vacuum drying mats; LVP over the wet area is lifted, dried and relaid. See water extraction.
  • Dry under the cabinets. Toe-kicks come off or small hidden ports go in, and dry air is pushed under the cabinet boxes.
  • Dry the hidden fridge wall. The baseboard inside the fridge alcove comes off (that wall is hidden once the fridge is back) and dry air is directed into the stud bays.

Appliance Leak FAQs

Can the wall behind my fridge be dried without opening it up?

Usually, yes. The baseboard inside the fridge alcove comes off, small ports go into the stud bay, and dry air dries the wall and the room behind it.

Why didn't water come out the front of my dishwasher?

In some remodeled kitchens the dishwasher sits lower than the finished floor, so water flows sideways under cabinets and flooring instead.

My hardwood is cupping near the fridge. Can it be saved?

Often, if drying mats go on within a day or two.

How long does appliance leak drying take?

Most appliance leaks dry in 3 to 5 days, or 5 to 8 days with hardwood mat drying.

What are signs of a hidden fridge or dishwasher leak?

Cupping or dark boards near the appliance, a musty smell, swollen baseboards or toe-kicks, soft flooring, or a water bill that crept up.

What ice maker line should I install next time?

Ask your plumber for a braided stainless steel line connected to a recessed quarter-turn valve box. Avoid plastic tubing and long coils of thin copper.

Can we use the kitchen during drying?

Usually, yes. The fridge may sit out of its alcove for a few days, but the rest of the kitchen stays usable. For urgent help, start with 24/7 emergency water damage cleanup.
