Water Emergency in Your West Plano Home? Do These 5 Things

  • Find which unit is leaking. Larger 75093 homes often have several water heaters and attic AC units. Shut down the leaking one if you can, not the whole house.
  • Close the main valve if needed. It's often in a mechanical room, a garage manifold cabinet, or one of the meter boxes at the curb (one may be irrigation).
  • Protect wood and stone floors. Blot water off white oak, walnut or travertine and lift silk or wool rugs so dyes don't bleed.
  • Don't let anyone pry off custom cabinets. Inset cabinetry can often be dried in place through the toe-kick.
  • Alert your gate. In gated sections, tell the guardhouse that a restoration truck is coming.

West Plano Water Damage FAQs

Can water under white oak floors be dried without tearing them out?

Often, yes, on clean-water leaks caught early. Vacuum drying mats pull water up through the seams and save many floors in place.

We have a hot-water recirculating pump. Why did we get a pinhole leak?

Recirculating loops keep hot water moving through copper around the clock. Combined with water chemistry and shifting clay, bends and fittings can wear faster.

How is the area behind wood paneling or wainscoting dried?

Thermal cameras map the wet area, then only the base shoe or a hidden spot behind the trim is opened to direct dry air inside.

Is documentation provided for high-value home insurers?

Yes. Thermal photos, daily drying logs and an itemized scope help support in-place drying of custom finishes.

Will drying equipment harm plaster, stone or fine finishes?

Drying is done gradually and checked daily so wood, plaster and stone dry evenly and reduce the risk of cracking or haze.

Are leaks in pool houses, guest casitas or outdoor kitchens handled?

Yes. Detached structures are dried the same way as the main house, with separate records for each building.

Can work be scheduled around household staff?

Yes. Daily check-ins and equipment placement can be coordinated with you or your house manager.
