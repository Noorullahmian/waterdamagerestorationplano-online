What Is AC Overflow Water Damage Cleanup?

AC overflow cleanup is the removal of condensate water, wet insulation and damaged materials after an attic air conditioner's drain pan overflows, followed by drying the ceiling, attic and walls below. It's separate from HVAC repair: your AC technician clears the drain, while water damage cleanup removes the water already in your home.

AC overflow at a glance:

  • Peak season in Plano: roughly May through October
  • Common causes: algae-clogged drain lines, blocked secondary drains, rusted pans
  • Water category: starts clean, but usually treated as Category 2 after sitting in the pan and passing through insulation
  • Ceiling drying time: often 2 to 3 days if caught before the drywall sags
  • Typical estimate: roughly $800 to $2,500 for a small ceiling stain with insulation removed

AC Leaking Into Your Ceiling? Do These 5 Things

  • Switch the thermostat from COOL to OFF. That stops the coil from making more water.
  • Set the fan to AUTO, not ON. A running blower can pull water from the pan into the ductwork.
  • Kill power to wet ceiling lights and vents. Overflow water runs along the outside of ducts and drips from registers and nearby lights.
  • Don't pour bleach into a fully clogged line. If the drain is plugged solid, adding liquid just overfills the pan faster.
  • Check the pan under the unit. If the pan under the attic air handler is full, it needs to be emptied before it spills or rusts through.

The Unit Sits in the Attic, Over Your Ceiling

In many Plano homes, the indoor coil and air handler sit in the attic over a drain pan, often above the main hallway, master bedroom or living room.

Humidity Means a Lot of Water

A typical home system can remove several gallons of water a day from humid North Texas air, and in July and August it barely stops running.

Two Drains, Two Ways to Fail

The primary drain usually runs into a plumbing drain. The secondary drain often exits at the soffit, sometimes over a window. When algae clogs the primary line, the pan fills. If insects block the soffit pipe or the pan has rusted through, the water goes onto your ceiling.

Why the Ceiling Keeps Getting Worse

Even after the drain is cleared, wet insulation keeps holding water against the ceiling, so damage continues until it's removed.

  • Wet insulation acts like a sponge on top of your drywall.
  • Attic heat doesn't dry it. The top of the insulation may dry, but the bottom stays wet against your cool ceiling.
  • It becomes gray water. After sitting in a slimy pan and passing through dusty insulation, it's usually treated as Category 2.
  • Water travels to walls. It runs along the ceiling to the nearest wall and drops inside, soaking baseboards a room away.

The Gap Your AC Tech Leaves, and How It Gets Filled

Your HVAC technician stops new water by clearing the drain, but the water already in your insulation, ceiling drywall and walls still needs to be removed and dried. Drying follows recognized restoration practices (IICRC S500 guidance).

  • Map the wet ceiling from below. Thermal cameras show the outline of wet drywall and insulation.
  • Clear the attic. Soaked insulation around and under the unit is removed, along with standing water in the pan and on the platform.
  • Dry the ceiling from above. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the exposed top of the drywall and joists. Caught before it sags, many ceilings dry in 2 to 3 days and only need stain-blocking primer and paint. See ceiling leak water damage.
  • Treat for mold. Joists, decking and the top of the drywall get antimicrobial treatment.
  • Check the walls below. Baseboards and wall cavities under the overflow are checked and dried if needed. See wall cavity drying.

Does Insurance Cover AC Overflow Damage in Texas?

A sudden overflow is often treated as accidental water damage under standard Texas homeowners policies, so cleanup and ceiling repair may be covered, while slow long-term leaks and the AC repair itself usually aren't. Coverage depends on your own policy.

AC Overflow FAQs

My AC technician cleared the drain. Why do I still need drying?

Clearing the drain stops new water. It doesn't remove what's already in your insulation, ceiling and walls. Left in place, wet insulation can sag the ceiling and grow mold within about 48 hours.

Why is water dripping right out of my ceiling vent?

Either the pan overflowed and water ran down the outside of the duct to the vent, or insulation pulled away from the vent box and hot attic air is condensing on the cold metal.

Why did the pan overflow if there's a drain going outside?

The outside drain is a backup. If insects blocked the soffit pipe or the pan rusted through, the water has nowhere to go but your ceiling.

Can the stain be dried without cutting a big hole?

Often, yes, if you call before the drywall sags. Removing the wet insulation and drying from above can save the ceiling in 2 to 3 days.

Is AC condensate water clean?

It starts clean, but after sitting in an algae-filled pan and passing through insulation it's usually treated as Category 2 and needs antimicrobial treatment.

What are the signs my AC drain is clogged?

Water in the secondary pan, water dripping from the soffit drain over a window, a musty smell near vents, or the system shutting off if a float switch is installed.

How do I stop this from happening again?

Ask your HVAC technician about a float switch that shuts the system off when the pan fills, drain line cleaning before summer, and replacing rusted pans. For urgent help, start with 24/7 emergency water damage cleanup.
