Emergency in Your Plano Home? Call First, Then Do This

  • Call (972) 472-7917. Stay on the line while you do the next steps if you can.
  • Shut off the water. Garage wall, flowerbed box or the City of Plano curb meter.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms. Never step into standing water near outlets.
  • Get out from under any sagging ceiling.
  • Take photos and video before you move anything.

What to Have Ready When You Call

These details help the crew arrive prepared. You don't need all of them.

  • Your address and ZIP code, plus gate codes or access notes for gated neighborhoods
  • Where the water came from, if known: pipe, water heater, AC, appliance, toilet, roof, storm water or sewage
  • Whether the water is still running and whether you found the shut-off. See the shut-off valve guide.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and whether a ceiling is sagging. See the sagging ceiling guide.
  • What kind of floors: carpet, hardwood, LVP or tile
  • Whether it's clean water, gray water or sewage
  • Your insurance company, if you plan to file a claim

What Happens After You Call

After you call, the process follows six steps.

  • Your call is answered with a few quick questions about what's happening.
  • A local water mitigation crew is contacted for your area.
  • The crew arrives and makes it safe. Water source confirmed off, power checked, ceilings handled.
  • Everything is measured and photographed. Thermal images and moisture readings before anything is moved.
  • You get a clear plan. What's wet, what can be saved, what has to go, and an estimate range.
  • Work starts right away. Extraction and drying begin on the same visit. See 24/7 emergency water damage cleanup.
