Legacy West & Deerfield Water Damage FAQs

An upstairs pipe or water heater leaked in our Deerfield home. How is the space between floors dried?

Thermal cameras map every wet bay between floors. Can light trims are removed or small hidden ports are drilled, then warm, dry air is directed in until framing and subfloor read dry.

Water ran down inside the shared wall of our townhome. Who handles it?

Both sides of the shared wall are checked with thermal cameras and probes, and separate drying records are kept for each owner and the HOA if needed.

Why do Kings Ridge hillside homes get water downstairs in storms?

Terraced lots slope toward the house. When heavy rain overwhelms yard drains, water pools against the uphill wall and can push through brick weep holes onto the slab.

Yes. Extraction and setup can happen in the evening so offices and retail floors are safer for staff and customers by morning.
