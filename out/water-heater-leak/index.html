What Is Water Heater Leak Cleanup?

Water heater leak cleanup is the removal of water, wet insulation and damaged materials after a tank or its fittings fail, followed by drying the attic, ceilings, walls and floors the water reached. It doesn't include replacing the tank, which is your plumber's job.

Water heater leak cleanup at a glance:

  • Most common in Plano: 40 to 50 gallon gas tanks installed in attics of two-story homes
  • Typical failure age: many tanks start failing around 8 to 12 years
  • Water category: usually treated as Category 2 because of rust, sediment and attic dust
  • Biggest risk: wet insulation weighing down and collapsing the ceiling below
  • Typical drying time: 3 to 5 days; 5 to 7 days for two-story floods
  • Typical estimate: roughly $3,000 to $8,000 for an attic tank with wet insulation and one ceiling

Water Heater Burst? Do These 5 Things Now

  • Close the cold-water valve on top of the tank. While it's open, city pressure keeps pushing water through the failed tank.
  • Turn off the gas or power. On gas units, turn the control to OFF or PILOT. On electric units, switch off the 240V breaker.
  • Drain it if you can reach it safely. If it's slowly filling the pan, run a hose from the bottom drain valve to a tub or outside to take weight off the platform.
  • Clear the room underneath. Attic tanks often sit over hallways, closets or bedrooms. Move people, pets and furniture away from any wet or bowing ceiling.
  • Leave lights and fans off below the tank. Water on top of ceiling drywall heads for recessed light housings first.

Why Attic Water Heaters Fail So Hard in Plano

Plano attic water heaters fail because of extreme attic temperatures, hard-water sediment and drain pans that can't handle a full-pressure leak. Across Central, North and West Plano (75023, 75024, 75025, 75075, 75093), many builders placed one or two tanks in the attic to save floor space.

Extreme Attic Temperatures

Attics swing from freezing in winter to well over 130°F in summer. That constant heating and cooling wears on tank welds, fittings and the anode rod that protects the steel.

Hard Water Builds Sediment

Minerals in North Texas water settle at the bottom of the tank. Sediment over the burner overheats the steel until the bottom can crack, and the water that comes out is rusty and dirty.

Drain Pans That Can't Keep Up

A pan and drain line are required under attic tanks. But a rusted pan, a blocked drain outlet, or a full-pressure split can overwhelm a small gravity drain quickly, sending water over the pan edge into the insulation. See ceiling leak water damage.

Attic Tank vs. Garage Tank: Two Different Floods

An attic tank floods from the top down; a garage tank floods from the bottom up.

Attic Water Heater: Top-Down Flood

Water soaks the attic platform, then the blown-in insulation. Wet insulation gets heavy fast, so the ceiling bows and can collapse within hours. Meanwhile water runs along joists and drops inside walls to the floors below.

Garage Water Heater: Bottom-Up Flood

In single-story ranch homes in East and Central Plano (75074, 75023), the tank sits on a raised platform in the garage corner. When it bursts, water runs under the garage-to-house wall and soaks hallway carpet, closets and laundry cabinets inside.

The Water Heater Flood Cleanup Process

Cleanup works from the attic down in five steps. Work follows recognized restoration practices (IICRC S500 guidance).

  • Make the ceiling safe and clear the attic. Standing water is vacuumed from the pan and platform, soaked insulation is removed, and bowing drywall is brought down safely before it falls.
  • Map every level. Attic water travels sideways along joists and drops down walls rooms away, so upstairs and downstairs ceilings, walls and baseboards are scanned with thermal cameras and probes.
  • Treat it as gray water. Rust and sediment usually make it Category 2, so floors and carpet are extracted, wet pad is removed, and surfaces get antimicrobial treatment. See water extraction.
  • Dry the attic and the space between floors. A wet platform and hidden wall cavities can stay damp for weeks if only the room below is dried. Air movers and dehumidifiers are placed on the attic decking and in the ceiling cavity.
  • Verify every level is dry. Readings on attic joists, upstairs subfloor and downstairs studs are logged daily until they reach about 12 to 15%. See structural drying.

Is a Burst Water Heater Covered by Insurance in Texas?

A sudden tank failure is usually treated as sudden and accidental water damage under standard Texas homeowners policies, so cleanup, drying and repairs are often covered, while the tank itself usually isn't because it failed from age. Coverage always depends on your own policy.

Water Heater Leak FAQs

Is water from a burst water heater clean or contaminated?

Water from the cold supply is clean, but water sitting at the bottom of an older tank carries rust and sediment, and water dripping through attic insulation picks up dust. It's usually treated as Category 2, which means antimicrobial treatment and pad removal.

My garage water heater leaked. Why are my hallway closet baseboards wet?

The tank sits against the house wall. Water runs down onto the bottom wood plate and wicks under it into closets and rooms on the other side.

Can my ceiling be saved after an attic tank burst?

Often, yes, if the wet insulation is pulled off the top of the drywall on day one. That weight is what brings ceilings down. If the ceiling is sagging, see sagging ceiling safety steps.

How long does drying take after a water heater flood?

Most attic tank floods dry in 3 to 5 days. Two-story floods with wet ceilings between floors can take 5 to 7 days.

What are the warning signs a water heater is about to fail?

Rust-colored hot water, rumbling or popping from the tank, moisture or rust around the base, a pan with water in it, and a tank older than about 10 years.

How can I stop this from happening again?

When the new tank goes in, ask your plumber about a heavy-duty pan and an automatic leak shut-off valve that closes the water line when the pan gets wet. Some homeowners also move the tank out of the attic during replacement.

Do you replace the water heater?

No. Your plumber replaces the tank; the water damage is handled here, timed so the new unit goes in on a dry platform. For urgent help, start with 24/7 emergency water damage cleanup.
