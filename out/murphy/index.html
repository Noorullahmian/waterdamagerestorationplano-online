Water Emergency in Your Murphy, Parker or Wylie Home? Do These 5 Things

  • Close the main valve. Usually in the garage or at the curb meter. On larger Parker properties, check for a separate valve near where the line enters the house. See where to find your shut-off valve.
  • Shut off the attic water heater or AC if that's the source. Many two-story homes here have both in the attic.
  • Turn off breakers to wet rooms. Especially where water reaches outlets or ceiling lights.
  • Treat outside water as contaminated. Runoff from open fields, creeks or patios is Category 3.
  • Photograph before you clean. Capture the source, the water line and the wet rooms.
