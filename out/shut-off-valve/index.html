Where Is the Main Water Shut-Off in a Plano Home?

In most Plano homes, the main water shut-off valve is on the garage wall facing the street, in a box near the front flowerbed, or at the City of Plano water meter in a box between the sidewalk and the street. The curb meter valve, closed with a curb key, shuts off water to the whole house.

Check in this order:

  • Garage wall facing the street, often near the water heater or a hose bib (common in homes built from the 1970s through the 2000s).
  • Front flowerbed box, a round or rectangular lid near the front of the house with a ball or gate valve inside.
  • City of Plano curb meter, the last resort, closed with a T-shaped curb key.

Step by Step: How to Shut Off Your Water

Shutting off your water takes eight quick steps.

  • Grab your phone light. Garages and meter boxes are dark.
  • Check the garage wall facing the street. Look for a pipe coming up from the floor with a round handle or lever.
  • Turn it the right way. A round handle turns clockwise until it stops. A lever turns a quarter turn so it sits across the pipe.
  • Check the flowerbed box if the garage has nothing. Lift the lid carefully and watch for wasps and spiders.
  • Go to the curb meter if needed. Lift the meter box lid. The shut-off is a small valve on the street side of the meter with a slot or tab.
  • Use a curb key. A long T-shaped meter key from a hardware store fits most meters. Turn a quarter turn until the slot runs across the pipe.
  • Open a low faucet. A downstairs tub or outside hose bib drains water still sitting in upstairs and attic pipes.
  • Call for cleanup. Turning off the water stops new water. It doesn't remove what's already in floors, walls and ceilings.

Faster Options for Certain Leaks

For many leaks, you can shut off just one fixture instead of the whole house.

Water Heater Leak

Close the cold-water valve on top of the tank. That stops the leak while leaving the rest of the house on. See water heater leak cleanup.

Warm Spot on the Floor

A warm patch on tile or wood often means a hot-water slab leak. Closing the cold valve on top of the water heater can isolate it. See warm spot on floor guide.

Toilet, Sink or Washer

Each usually has its own small valve behind or below it. Turn it clockwise to stop just that fixture.

Fridge Ice Maker

In many kitchens, the ice maker line has its own small valve under the kitchen sink. See appliance leak cleanup.

What Not to Do

  • Don't force an old valve with a wrench. Brass gate valves in older homes can snap and make things worse.
  • Don't step into standing water near outlets. Turn off power to wet rooms first.
  • Don't wait for a plumber before calling for cleanup. The plumber fixes the pipe; drying should start as soon as the water is off. See water extraction.

Do This Now, Before You Ever Need It

  • Find your main valve today and make sure it turns.
  • Buy a curb key and keep it in the garage.
  • Put a bright tag on the valve so anyone in the house can find it.
  • Learn where the valve is on every water heater and toilet.
  • Ask your plumber about replacing an old gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve.

Plano Shut-Off Valve FAQs

Where is the main water shut-off valve in a Plano house?

Usually on the garage wall facing the street, in a box near the front flowerbed, or at the City of Plano meter at the curb.

What tool turns off the City of Plano water meter?

A curb key, also called a meter key, sold at most hardware stores. It's a long T-handle that reaches into the meter box.

Is it OK to turn off the water at the street myself?

Homeowners commonly close the curb valve in an emergency. If you're unsure or the box is damaged, contact the City of Plano water department.

Which way do I turn the water shut-off valve?

Round handles turn clockwise ("righty-tighty") until they stop. Lever handles turn a quarter turn so the lever sits across the pipe.

My valve won't turn. What now?

Don't force it. Go to the curb meter instead, or call for help.

How do I turn off water to just the water heater?

Close the cold-water valve on the pipe going into the top of the tank, and turn off its gas or power.

I shut off the water. Am I done?

No. The water already in your floors, walls and ceilings still needs to come out. Call (972) 472-7917 for extraction and drying. See structural drying and 24/7 emergency water damage cleanup.
