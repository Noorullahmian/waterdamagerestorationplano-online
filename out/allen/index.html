Water Emergency in Your Allen Home? Do These 5 Things

  • Close the cold-water valve on attic water heaters. Many two-story homes in Twin Creeks and StarCreek have attic tanks.
  • Isolate a hot-water slab leak at the tank. Shutting the water heater valve can stop a hot-line slab leak while keeping cold water on.
  • Shut the curb meter for a burst pipe. The meter box near the front curb closes with a curb key.
  • Turn off breakers to wet rooms. Don't leave lamps, power strips or ceiling fans running in wet areas.
  • Blot wood floors and open sink cabinets. Especially in homes with hardwood.

Allen Water Damage FAQs

A plumber fixed our slab leak in Twin Creeks. Why do we need drying too?

Plumbers fix the pipe. Removing water already trapped under floors and cabinets takes drying mats, dehumidifiers and thermal cameras.

Why did water come through our ceiling on a sunny 100°F day?

An attic AC pulls gallons of water from the air each day. When its drain clogs and the pan overflows, water goes into the insulation and through the ceiling.

Can cabinets be dried without removing the granite?

Usually, yes, on clean-water leaks. The toe-kick comes off or hidden ports go in, and dry air is pushed under the cabinets.

What if creek runoff came under our back door?

Outside water is Category 3. Affected pad and lower drywall come out, wall plates are cleaned and disinfected, and framing is dried.

Are restaurants and shops near Watters Creek covered?

Yes. After-hours extraction and drying help businesses reopen as fast as possible.

Our east Allen home is from the 1980s. Is a slab leak likely?

Older copper under the slab on expansive clay can fail with age. Watch for warm floor spots, damp grout and water bill jumps. See moisture mapping.
