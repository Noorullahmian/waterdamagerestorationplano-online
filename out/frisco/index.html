Water Emergency in Your Frisco Home? Do These 5 Things

  • Use your PEX manifold if you have one. Many newer Frisco homes have a manifold in the garage or utility room where each fixture line can be shut off separately.
  • Shut off attic water heaters or tankless units. Two-story homes often have more than one.
  • Check upstairs game-room wet bars and media rooms. They often sit above a first-floor bedroom or family room.
  • Blot engineered hardwood and lift rugs. Wide-plank wood glued to the slab cups quickly if water sits in the seams.
  • Take photos first. Capture the source and wet rooms before anything moves.

Frisco Water Damage FAQs

Our Frisco home is only five years old. Why did a pipe leak?

In newer subdivisions, the clay under the house goes through its biggest movement in the first several years, which can stress plumbing connections, shower pans and wall fittings.

An upstairs washer or game-room bar leaked. How is the ceiling below dried?

Thermal cameras trace wet bays between floors, can light trims come off, and dry air is pushed through the cavity until framing reads dry.

Can glued engineered wood in Starwood or Richwoods be dried?

Often, if drying starts within 24 to 48 hours of a clean leak, before the glue lets go.

What about Frisco Lakes and single-story homes?

Single-story homes with continuous hard floors let water spread fast across one level, so quick extraction and clear insurance records matter most.

Our home is still under builder warranty. Should we call the builder first?
