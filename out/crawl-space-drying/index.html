What Is Crawl Space Drying? (Quick Answer)

Crawl space drying is the removal of standing water, wet insulation and debris from under a pier-and-beam home, followed by drying the joists, beams and subfloor with air movers and dehumidifiers until wood reaches normal moisture levels. It prevents rot, mold, floor cupping and musty odors inside the home.

Crawl space drying at a glance:

  • Plano homes affected: mainly older pier-and-beam homes in East Plano (75074)
  • Common causes: burst pipes, drain and sewer leaks, storm water, summer humidity
  • Mold and rot risk: wood left wet above about 20% moisture
  • Typical drying time: 3 to 7 days
  • Typical estimate: roughly $1,500 to $4,000 for a small clean-water leak in part of the crawl space

Water Under Your Pier-and-Beam Home? Do These 5 Things

  • Turn off the main breaker before looking under the house. Wiring and junction boxes run through crawl spaces. Never enter a wet crawl space with power on.
  • Shut off the water if a pipe broke. Use the house valve or the City of Plano curb meter.
  • Don't crawl into standing water. Crawl spaces can hold sewage, wildlife and sharp debris.
  • Close floor vents inside if they smell musty. That slows damp crawl space air from entering the house.
  • Photograph what you can see. A photo from the crawl space opening is enough to show water depth for your claim.

Why Crawl Spaces Get Wet in Plano

Plano crawl spaces get wet from four main sources: frozen or burst pipes, drain and sewer leaks, storm water, and summer humidity.

Frozen and Burst Pipes Under the House

Supply lines often run exposed through vented crawl spaces. In a hard freeze like Winter Storm Uri, they can split and empty onto the soil. See burst pipe cleanup.

Drain and Sewer Leaks

Old cast-iron and clay drain lines crack, and tree roots push into joints. Sewage in a crawl space is Category 3. See sewage backup cleanup.

Storm Water and Poor Grading

Heavy rain, clogged gutters or a yard that slopes toward the house can fill a crawl space with water that has nowhere to go. See flood cleanup.

Summer Humidity

Even without a leak, humid air can condense on cool pipes and the underside of air-conditioned floors, keeping the crawl space damp all summer.

What Water Does Under a Pier-and-Beam House

Water in a crawl space damages floors, insulation, indoor air and wood framing.

  • Floors cup and sag. Wet joists and subfloor swell, and older oak and pine floors above can cup and squeak.
  • Insulation falls. Wet batts between the joists sag and drop, holding water against the wood.
  • Musty air comes inside. Crawl space air is pulled up through floor gaps and vents.
  • Wood rots and mold grows. Joists and sill beams left above about 20% moisture can grow mold and eventually rot.

The Crawl Space Drying Process

Crawl space drying is handled as confined-space work in six steps. Work follows recognized restoration practices (IICRC S500 guidance).

  • Make it safe. Power is confirmed off, the space is checked for gas, sewage and hazards, and technicians enter in protective gear.
  • Pump out standing water. Low-profile pumps remove water from the soil and low spots. See water extraction.
  • Remove wet insulation and debris. Soaked batts come out so the wood can dry.
  • Clean and treat. If sewage or storm water was involved, joists and soil surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial.
  • Dry the wood from below. Air movers and ducted dehumidifiers run until joists, beams and subfloor reach dry standard, logged daily. Floors above may also need hardwood floor drying.
  • Protect it going forward. A heavy vapor barrier over the soil helps keep ground moisture out once the space is dry.

Crawl Space FAQs

Can water be pumped out from under my house?

Yes. Low-profile pumps, crawl space air movers and ducted dehumidifiers are used for pier-and-beam homes.

Why does my house smell musty?

Damp crawl space air rises into the house through floor gaps and vents. Drying the crawl space and adding a vapor barrier usually helps clear it.

My floors are cupping but nothing leaked inside. Why?

Moisture from a wet crawl space is soaking the underside of the floor. The fix is drying from below, not just above.

Is water in a crawl space dangerous?

It can be. Live wiring, sewage and wildlife are common. Stay out and let trained technicians in protective gear handle it.

How long does crawl space drying take?

Usually 3 to 7 days, depending on how wet the soil and wood are and how much air can move through the space.

Should I install a vapor barrier after drying?

A heavy vapor barrier over the soil is one of the most effective ways to keep ground moisture out of a dried crawl space.
