What We Stand For

Measured, Not Guessed

Drying follows IICRC S500 guidance. Thermal cameras and moisture meters are used before, during and after drying, and equipment leaves only when readings show your home is dry. See structural drying.

Save First, Remove Last

Hardwood is dried in place with vacuum mats, cabinets through hidden toe-kick ports, and walls through holes the baseboard covers. Removal happens only when water category, materials or safety require it. See hardwood floor drying.

Honest About Costs

No exact price is quoted before moisture is measured, because that number would be a guess. You get honest estimate ranges up front and a real scope after inspection. See the water damage cost guide.

Honest About Insurance

No one can promise your claim will be paid. What you get is photos, thermal images, daily logs and an itemized scope that help your adjuster see what happened. See the Texas insurance guide.

Clear About What's Not Included

Water damage crews dry and restore water-damaged structures. They don't fix pipes, replace water heaters or repair roofs. Your plumber or roofer handles those, and work is coordinated so nothing waits.

How a Job Works, Start to Finish

A typical job follows seven steps.
